Lateral inhibition: why edges pop

Now answer without rewatching

Say or write each answer before you open it.

1. Define lateral inhibition in one sentence.

Activity in one neuron reduces the activity of its neighbors (slide 24).

2. Which retinal cells produce it, and which cells do they inhibit?

Horizontal cells, inhibiting bipolar cells (slide 22).

3. In the video's numbers, why does the bright cell at the edge send 8.8 while the deep bright cells send 8?

It has one dim neighbor, so it loses 0.1 x (10 + 2) = 1.2 instead of 0.1 x (10 + 10) = 2.

4. What does lateral inhibition do for perception?

Sharpens contrast at the borders of objects, so edges stand out.

5. Why do the white crossroads in the Hermann grid look gray?

A crossroad has white on four sides, so it gets more inhibition than a street with white on two sides.
